Privacy Policy

Clerra · by Motions Studio · Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Clerra app and service ("Clerra", "we", "us") collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Clerra is a work app for cleaning and home-service companies: company owners use it to schedule jobs and manage their team, team members use it to record their work with checklists and photos, and the company's clients can see their visits and reports.

1. Who is responsible for your information

  • Your account (name, email, phone, password, profile photo) and subscriptions: Motions Studio is responsible for this information.
  • Information a company puts into Clerra (its clients, properties, jobs, photos, reports and team): the cleaning company that uses Clerra decides what is recorded and is responsible for it. We store and process it on the company's behalf and only to provide the service. If you are a team member or a client of a company, please contact that company first with questions about the information it holds about you; we will help them respond.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, a password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form) and an optional profile photo.
  • Company details: company name, logo, country, time zone and contact details.
  • Work information entered by a company and its team: client names and contact details, property names and addresses, access instructions and entry codes, job schedules, checklists and tasks, notes, issues and damage reports, booking requests, and visit reports (PDF).
  • Photos taken or imported in the app for a job (for example before and after photos). Photos are reduced in size on your phone, and location and camera details stored inside the image file are removed before upload. Imported photos are labelled as imported.

Information collected while you use the app

  • Location, only at check-in and check-out: when a team member starts or finishes a visit, the app records their location once, if they allow it, so the company can see the visit took place at the property. Clerra never tracks location in the background and the app works if location is turned off.
  • Times of actions: when tasks are completed, photos taken, visits started, submitted and approved, and who did them. This is shown to the company as a record of the work.
  • Notification token: an identifier for your device so we can send you notifications (for example a new job or a report). You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Technical information: app version, and basic server logs (such as IP address and time of a request) kept for security and to fix problems.

Information from others

  • Purchases: subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play and managed with RevenueCat. We receive the subscription status (plan, renewal and expiry dates). We never receive or store your card or bank details.

We do not use advertising, we do not show ads, we do not sell personal information, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking that follows you across other apps or websites. We do not use your information to train AI models.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service: accounts, scheduling, checklists, photo evidence, reports, notifications, client access and booking requests.
  • To keep the service safe: security, preventing abuse and fraud, and keeping an activity record of important changes.
  • To manage subscriptions and apply the limits of each plan.
  • To support you and to tell you about important changes to the service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Where privacy laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on: performing our contract with you or your company; our legitimate interests in running a secure, reliable service; your consent where we ask for it (for example location or notifications, which you can withdraw in your phone's settings at any time); and legal obligations.

4. Who can see the information

  • Inside a company: owners and managers can see the company's jobs, photos, reports, team activity and check-in locations. Team members see the jobs assigned to them. Clients see only their own properties, their visits and the reports the company sends them — never access codes, internal notes or precise check-in locations.
  • Service providers who process information for us under contract, only to run Clerra:
    • Namecheap — web hosting and database.
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — delivering notifications.
    • RevenueCat — managing subscriptions.
    • Apple App Store and Google Play — app distribution and payments.
  • When required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer (in which case this policy continues to apply).

5. International transfers

Clerra is used internationally, and our hosting and service providers may process information in countries other than yours (for example the United States). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account and company information is kept while the account is active.
  • When a company owner deletes their account, the company and all of its data (jobs, properties, clients, photos, reports) are deleted.
  • When a team member or client deletes their account, their personal account details are deleted; work records they created belong to the company and stay with it, shown as "Deleted user".
  • Copies in server backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.
  • Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app (Profile settings → Delete account), or by emailing us. See how to delete your account.
  • Access, correct or export your information: you can update your name, phone and photo in the app, and ask us for a copy or correction.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California, Canada, Australia or Brazil), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.
  • Permissions: camera, photos, location and notifications are only used when you allow them and can be turned off in your phone's settings.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We may need to confirm your identity first.

8. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, photos and reports are only available to signed-in people with permission to see them, and there are no public links to them. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected companies and users of a data breach as required by law.

9. Children

Clerra is a business app for adults. It is not directed at children and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children; if you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email before they apply.
