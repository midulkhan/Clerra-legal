Delete your Clerra account

Clerra · by Motions Studio. You can delete your account at any time, directly in the app.

In the app

  1. Open Clerra and sign in.
  2. Owners and managers: open More. Team members and clients: tap your profile picture.
  3. Tap Profile settings, then Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address, phone number, password, profile photo and notification settings are erased permanently.
  • If you own a company account: the company and all of its data are deleted permanently — jobs, properties, clients, photos, reports, schedules and team access.
  • If you are a team member or client: the visit records you helped create belong to the cleaning company and are kept by them, shown as “Deleted user”.
  • Copies in server backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, after which no copy remains.
